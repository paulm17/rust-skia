### Test-source formatting/lint normalization

The first quality-gate run revealed defects confined to Codex-owned M06 test files (production `src/` was clean). `cargo fmt --all -- --check` reported differences in all ten test files (`tests/m06_test.rs` plus `tests/m06/{bezier,cubic_map,geometry,insetconvexpoly,mono_x,mono_y,offsetsimplepoly,polyutils,supplemental}.rs`); `cargo clippy --all-targets --locked -- -D warnings` failed with 11 errors: unused imports `rust_skia::sk_geometry::*` and `rust_skia::sk_poly_utils::*` (`tests/m06_test.rs:20-21`), unnecessary parentheses (`tests/m06/insetconvexpoly.rs:43,56`, `tests/m06/offsetsimplepoly.rs:49,62`) and non-upper-case constant names (`tests/m06/geometry_fixtures.rs:2,8,14,22,28`). With the test owner's approval the fixes were purely non-semantic: `cargo fmt --all` reflowed whitespace only, and the harness crate-level allow-list gained `unused_imports`, `unused_parens` and `non_upper_case_globals`. The complete diff is `m06-test-format.diff`; before/after hashes are `test-hashes-preformat.txt`/`test-hashes-postformat.txt` and the final `test-hashes-final.txt`. No assertion, expected value, tolerance, fixture or exclusion changed — every registration's reporter assertion count is identical before and after (e.g. `CubicMap` 319,375; `SkBezierCubic_CheckTRange` 10,000), and all behavioral, formatting, Clippy and validator gates were rerun on the final tree.

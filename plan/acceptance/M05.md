### Production fix during acceptance

The only production defect found by execution was `src/matrix_utils.rs::is_degenerate_2x2`, which previously used a relative-to-sum-of-squares threshold and misclassified anisotropic scales such as `(0.001, 5000)` as degenerate, breaking 14 decomposition assertions in the original `Matrix` registration. It now matches pinned `SkMatrix.cpp:177-181`: `SkScalarNearlyZero(scaleX*scaleY - skewX*skewY, SK_ScalarNearlyZero*SK_ScalarNearlyZero)`. The targeted `Matrix` registration then passed all 3,022,797 assertions; no test was changed.
